# Named marks and grip occlusion

The first count review incorrectly required every construction mark to be exposed
above the hand. Native close-ups and `GRIPS`/`NAMED_ART` show that lower marks are
occluded by the gripping fingers. Staff of the Ninth Circle has nine authored
cross-bands, Ogre's Toll five and Boarspear three; their full counts are not all
visible in the actual held reference. The original paintings reproduce that
visible construction. Do not move hands or stretch shafts merely to expose
hidden marks.

The attempted Ninth Circle and Ogre's Toll full-exposure edits are retained as
rejected provenance. The proposed Boarspear edit was cancelled before generation.
Original sources remain selected for all three families. This review records the
visible native match, not a claim to inspect concealed painted construction.
