All square, opaque64 textures; retain256 detail sources/reviews. Exact authored
full-panel crops; no creature anchors, padding, alpha openings or newly painted
routes. Floor/roof need tile/repeat review, eight cached Uint32 shade levels and
existing low texel density. Mosaic has inset diamond; altar slab has worn circular
groove/short marks, not new powers. Water stays blue and shallow over flags, not
solid marsh mud; rubble remains small chips; straw old muted gold, not bright hay.

Rock pillar is a full surface with cylinder shading, not an isolated silhouette.
Fallen wall remains rubble-filled opaque obstruction, not a passage. Wet/dry
fountain shares basin, niche and spout positions; dry has no stream or blue water.
Torch frames share wall, bracket and handle with distinct upright/left-tall/right-
short flames. Keep renderer cadence, Calm handling and existing light sources.

Match mature gritty painted monster/prop art and O Grey Halls stone. Preserve
originals, exact prompts/references/repairs and generator paths. Scripts may only
capture, crop, scale and assemble reviews, never paint/recolour/seam-synthesize.
Review native size, detail, tiling, near/far floor/wall/ceiling use, fountain states
and torch motion. Retain procedural reuse/fallback and region-specific materials
until coordinated integration. No shipped loader, service worker or rules changes.
