# Batch O — base dungeon material sets

Thirteen existing themes × wall/floor/ceiling =39 material tiles. Source/review only. Preserve unpublished M/N work. Native64 opaque export plus 256px material review tiles; eight cached floor/ceiling darkness levels, no sprite anchors.

Audit: assets.js TEX64, FLOOR_LEVELS8, regionart baked details and looks.js seven floor/four ceiling/three wall kinds. Renderer wraps power-of-two texels, shades existing light/fog and softens cached distant detail beyond3 tiles. Baseline wall selection includes alternate/cracked/damp/decor variants; existing doors/locks/arches/stairs/cell gates/fountains have state-bearing geometry. This pass commissions baseline materials for all themes only; retain original state art and non-baseline kinds. No lock colour, opening, collision, secret wall, water/pool or new light cues may be changed. Do not claim all architecture artwork complete.

Review 3×3 repeats, numeric edge discontinuity, native64/detail, all thirteen themes at both phone widths in a controlled material booth. Keep preview-only proxy substitution distinct from integration. No gameplay/runtime edits. Native offline reload and isolated frame-rate checks are integration gates; source exports and proxy previews do not establish them. No failed seam result may be presented as seamless; repair/review obvious seams or document limitations.
