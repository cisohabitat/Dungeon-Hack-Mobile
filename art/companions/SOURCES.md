The preview covers all six kinds in companion.js. Appearance follows folk.js
and the recruitment descriptions: a brown floppy-eared hound with red collar,
a grey collarless wolf, a green rag-clad goblin, a kettle-hatted sellsword,
a grey-green hooded mender and an amber-eyed dark elf renegade in brown leather.
These are type portraits; names vary between runs. The direction emphasized
natural anatomy, quiet backgrounds, readable faces and physically connected
equipment in the approved hero painting style. This is a direction summary,
not an exact prompt transcript.

The original sheet is preserved. generation.json records label-free square
crop coordinates. The shared portrait exporter converts these to 512-pixel
WebP files at quality 85; upsampling adds no generated detail. Only compressed
exports ship. Run `python3 art/portraits/export.py --check` to verify them.
