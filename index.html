How to Play

You are alone in a dungeon generated from a seed. Descend every level, find the Heart of the Mountain on the deepest floor, and take it from the thing that guards it.

Moving

The d-pad turns you and steps you one square at a time. Walk into doors to open them, into locked doors to use a matching key, and into a staircase to take it. Hold a button to keep walking. You can also swipe on the view: left or right to turn, up or down to step. Tap the view to act on what is in front of you.

Secrets

Some walls hide secret doors leading to treasure vaults. Walk into a suspicious wall or press Use facing it to search. Thieves often notice them just by walking past. Fountains set into the walls restore you fully, once.

Fighting

Everything happens in real time. Attack strikes the square in front of you. Monsters that reach you will hit back; step away, close doors behind you, or fight in corridors where only one can reach you. Thieves deal double damage to sleeping foes.

Magic

Mages and Clerics have spell points that return slowly as you walk, or fully when you Rest. Cast repeats your last spell. Anyone can read scrolls from their pack.

Survival

Walking makes you hungry. Eat rations from your pack or you will start to starve. Resting heals you fully but costs food and is impossible with enemies close by. Watch for traps in corridors.

Keyboard

W/S or arrows to move, A/D to turn, Q/E to sidestep, Space to attack, U to use, C to cast, R to rest, M map, I pack, P spells, H hero, Esc to close.

Progress is saved automatically when you change levels. Save any time from the menu. Permadeath deletes your save when you die.
